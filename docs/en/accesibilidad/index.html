Accessibility

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Our commitment

At White Fox Energy we want this site to be usable by anyone, regardless of ability or device. We work to meet AA level of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1.

Measures in place

  • AA-level colour contrast on text and controls.
  • Full keyboard navigation, including the kits carousel.
  • Alt text on images and ARIA labels on interactive components.
  • Semantic heading structure and valid HTML on every page.
  • Legible, functional content on mobile, tablet and desktop.

Ran into a barrier?

If anything on this site is hard to use or inaccessible, write to us at info@wf-energy.com describing the issue and the page where it occurs. We commit to reviewing and fixing it as soon as possible.
