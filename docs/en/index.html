Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

About solar panels, grants, batteries, chargers, electrical certificates and home automation in Alicante and Murcia.

Today you can access the MOVES III programme (up to €1,300 per charging point), IDAE self-consumption aid and grants from the Valencian Community and Murcia Region governments. In many cases the state tax deduction reaches 20% on income tax (IRPF). We advise you at no cost on every aid applicable to your case.

A standard residential installation (6–10 panels) is completed in 1–2 working days. Housing communities or industrial installations can take 3 to 5 days. The subsequent administrative paperwork takes 4 to 8 weeks.

It depends on your consumption profile. If you consume a lot at night or want full grid independence, a battery maximises savings. If your consumption is mostly during the day, panels alone already deliver an excellent return. We analyse it for free.

With a well-sized installation, the average saving in Alicante and Murcia ranges from 60% to 80% of the annual electricity bill. With a battery you can reach 90–95%. Typical payback is 5–7 years.

The surplus is fed into the grid and you get financial compensation on your bill (self-consumption with surplus). You can also store it in a battery to use it at night.

Yes, it's one of the most profitable combinations. You can charge your electric vehicle directly with the energy your panels produce, reducing charging cost to virtually zero during the sunniest months.

For single-family homes, notifying the DSO is usually enough. Listed buildings or communities may require additional permits. We handle every paperwork step for you.

Photovoltaic panels need minimal maintenance: an annual cleaning and a connection check. We offer maintenance plans starting at €79/year with performance guarantee.

Our home-automation systems integrate with any solar installation we build. We control lighting, climate, blinds, security and consumption from a single iOS and Android compatible app.
